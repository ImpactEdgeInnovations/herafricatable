## Introduction (proposed)

The Founding Table is a small, welcoming gathering for women who want to meet
with intention, share what they are building and find useful ways to support
one another. We will make space for thoughtful introductions and honest
conversation, with no pressure to pitch or perform. Come as you are; leave
with a few meaningful connections and a clearer idea of what to explore next.
